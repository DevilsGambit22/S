ANHS RADIO MUSIC FOLDER

Drop or upload any .mp3 files into this exact folder:
assets/music/

Examples:
  classroom-of-the-elite-theme.mp3
  white-room.mp3
  tokyo-night.mp3

After you commit the MP3 files to GitHub, the included workflow automatically scans this folder and rebuilds data/playlist.json.

You do not manually edit playlist.json.

Optional matching cover artwork:
  assets/music/tokyo-night.mp3
  assets/images/tokyo-night.png

The MP3 filename becomes the track title automatically.
